Notepad Forge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Notepad Forge does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not contact any server of ours.

Your documents

Notepad Forge opens only the files and folders you choose. Your documents stay on your Mac (or in the iCloud Drive folders you use) and are never uploaded by the app.

Encrypted text

When you encrypt text, the password is used on your Mac only to derive the key. It is never stored or sent anywhere, and it cannot be recovered.

Network

The Markdown preview can show images your document links to on the web. Those images are loaded directly from the addresses in your document. Nothing about you or your device is sent by Notepad Forge.

Settings

Your preferences, macros and custom languages are stored only on your Mac.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
